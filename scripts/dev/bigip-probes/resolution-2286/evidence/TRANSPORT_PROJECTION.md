# Retained transport projection

The historical captures in this subtree use synthetic documentation-range addresses and a synthetic device label. Transport-only references were projected consistently through summaries, fixture configuration, raw transcripts, encoded copies and archives. Archive owner labels are omitted. The projection does not change Tcl naming payloads, completion codes or naming observations.

Projected transport files and rebuilt archives must not be presented as the exact historical transport bytes. Release versions, interpreter versions, reached contexts and observed Tcl results remain as recorded. Captured scripts and their results belong in the [per-script evidence format](../../../../../docs/design/contracts/tcl-execution-evidence.md); these transport artifacts are development material.
