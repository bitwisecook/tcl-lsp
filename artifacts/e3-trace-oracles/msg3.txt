fix(trace): remove the newest duplicate, and order namespace-teardown unsets

Two more instances of #1440's root cause, found by attacking the diff with
fresh differential vectors against tclsh 8.6.16 and 9.0.4.

`trace remove` (and `trace vdelete`) deleted the OLDEST of several identical
registrations; C breaks at the first match walking its list head to tail, and
that head is the newest. The choice is observable twice over — in the firing
order of the survivors and in `trace info`. Our Vecs are oldest-first, so the
four removal sites become `rposition`: the VM's `remove_var_trace` and
`remove_cmd_trace`, and the runtime's variable and command/execution removal
branches.

Namespace teardown in the runtime fired a variable's unset traces oldest-first,
because it collects them with `retain`. Reversed, matching every other trace
list; the order *across* the namespace's variables is C's hash walk and stays
unpinned. The command `delete` traces collected alongside them get the same
treatment.

Regression vectors: two in the VM's tclsh-diffed table (variable, then command
and execution), and two runtime unit tests.
