# Purity domains: coverage matrix

Generated by `cargo xtask semantics-matrix` (issue #1712); do not edit.

A call is pure at a release where its side effects, world effects, completion and result stability are all `Complete` there and closed: no effect, no world access, only `Ok` or `Error`, and a referentially transparent result (`tcl_registry::purity::PurityDomains`). `Unknown` claims nothing and `Partial` is a lower bound, so neither is ever pure. A domain is `Complete` only for the release windows its implementation's source evidences (`rust/tcl-registry/tests/data/command_evidence`, written by `scripts/dev/tcl_command_evidence.py` and `scripts/dev/tcl_script_evidence.py`).

## Disposition of authored purity descriptors

| descriptor | disposition |
| --- | --- |
| the `PURE` trait | retired; derived from the four domains (`CommandSpec::is_pure_at`, `InvocationFacts::is_pure`). `RETIRED_TRAITS` names the replacement and the retired-API gate refuses the spelling. |
| `SubCommand::pure` | retired with the trait; a subcommand's purity derives from its own or its command's domains (`SubCommand::is_pure_at`). A pack's `pure` row is dropped with a notice. |
| stub `-pure` (`DeclaredCommand::pure`) | retained, irreducible: the document is the authority for a command it declares itself, and states its purity rather than evidencing a shipped implementation. |
| `Traits::PURE_EVALUATION` | retained, irreducible: a braced `expr` evaluates without effects of its own, while the calls inside it are each asked separately; it is not a footprint of the command. |
| `Traits::CSE_CANDIDATE` | retained: chooses which pure calls the optimiser commons and the redundant-call check reports; purity is necessary, not sufficient (`InvocationFacts::is_cse_pure`). |
| `CommandSpec::CLOSED_REFERENTIALLY_TRANSPARENT` | retained as an authoring base: it states the world effects and result stability as `Partial`, a lower bound, and closes nothing without evidence. |
